# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in DocMend's issues, pull
requests, discussions and other spaces run by Teyrin.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional, and stick to the topic.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- Respect confidentiality: do not share non-public Teyrin information,
  credentials or other people's personal data.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexual or violent content.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, or posting code you do not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

Teyrin moderates these spaces and may edit or remove content, lock threads,
or block anyone who breaks this code, at its sole discretion. Report problems
to the repository maintainers through GitHub.

Taking part does not grant any right to use DocMend; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
